# [Constant name]

## Description of constant

The smallest $e$ such that matrix multiplication of two $n \times n$ can be done in $O(n ^ e)$.
Technically to make this well-defined, $e$ is the infimum of all such exponents.

## Additional comments

- $O(n ^ 2)$ is not achievable. The best known lower bound for matrix-multiplication complexity is $\Omega(n2 log(n))$ as shown in [R2002].
